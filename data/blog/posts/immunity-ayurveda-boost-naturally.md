Immunity has become a health buzzword — but Ayurveda has been talking about it for millennia, under a precise name: **Ojas**. Ojas is the essence of all the tissues, the "finest end product" of good digestion. It is what gives the body stamina, resistance, and a glowing resilience. When Ojas is high, infections are rare and recovery is quick. When it is depleted, the body is vulnerable and slow to heal.

## What Builds Ojas — and What Drains It

### Builders of Ojas

1. **Strong digestion** — Ojas begins with *Ahara rasa*, the nutrient essence created when food is properly digested. Weak Agni means poor Ojas, no matter how good the food.
2. **Sound sleep** — Ojas is replenished at night.
3. **Regular, moderate routine** — the body thrives on rhythm.
4. **Calm mind** — chronic stress is a direct Ojas depleter.
5. **Sattvic food** — fresh, warm, pure, and nourishing.

### Drainers of Ojas

- Overeating, and eating late or irregularly.
- Excess stimulants: caffeine, sugar, alcohol.
- Chronic sleep deprivation.
- Chronic stress and anxiety.
- Over-exercise or a completely sedentary life.
- Excessive screen time and sensory overload.

## The Daily Immunity Routine

### Morning

1. **Warm water** with lemon on waking — gently stimulates digestion.
2. **Abhyanga** — a 5-minute warm oil self-massage grounds the day and supports the nervous system.
3. **Breathing practice** — 5–10 minutes of Anulom Vilom balances the nervous and immune systems.

### Daytime

4. **Ginger, turmeric, and honey** — a teaspoon of warm ginger-turmeric water (with honey when cool) is the classic daily immunity tonic.
5. **Sunlight** — 15 minutes of morning sun for vitamin D.
6. **Regular meals**, lunch as the largest meal.

### Evening

7. **Early, light dinner**.
8. **Warm milk with turmeric** — the classic Ojas builder, especially in cooler months.
9. **Sleep by 10:30 PM** — non-negotiable for immune recovery.

## Immunity Herbs (The "Four Guardians")

- **Guduchi (Giloy)** — the most famous immune herb in Ayurveda; supports the body's defense and is used widely during seasonal changes.
- **Ashwagandha** — supports the stress-immune axis.
- **Amla (Indian gooseberry)** — the richest natural source of vitamin C; the base of *Chyawanprash*.
- **Tulsi (Holy basil)** — the everyday immunity herb; a few leaves chewed daily or as tea.

**Chyawanprash** — the classical Rasayana jam made with amla and 40+ herbs — is the single most famous Ojas formula. A teaspoon daily, ideally in the morning on an empty stomach, is a traditional immune-building habit for adults and children alike.

## The Seasonal Reset: Ritucharya

Ayurveda teaches that immunity follows the seasons, and that small seasonal adjustments prevent illness:

- **Spring (Kapha season)** — lighten the diet; favor bitter and astringent tastes; this is the classic season for *Panchakarma* cleansing.
- **Summer (Pitta season)** — favor cooling, sweet, and liquid foods; hydrate well.
- **Autumn/Winter (Vata season)** — favor warm, oily, nourishing foods; protect the ears and head from cold.
- **Monsoon** — favor warm, light, easily digestible food; avoid raw salads and fasting.

## When the Season of Colds Arrives

During flu and cold season, add:

- **Tulsi-ginger tea** through the day.
- A daily **steam inhalation** with a few drops of eucalyptus (if no allergy).
- **Haldi (turmeric) milk** at night.
- **Nasya** — a drop of warm medicated ghee or oil in each nostril, a classical Ayurvedic practice that protects the respiratory tract (use prepared nasal oils, not plain ghee, and not during an active infection without guidance).

## The Honest Part

No herb, however classical, makes an unhealthy lifestyle immune. The power of the Ayurvedic approach is that it builds resilience daily, through food, sleep, rhythm, and a calm mind — with herbs as a supporting cast. That combination is what actually keeps you well through the seasons.
