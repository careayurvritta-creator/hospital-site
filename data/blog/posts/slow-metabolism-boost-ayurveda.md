Every body has a metabolic thermostat — a rate at which it burns fuel. Ayurveda calls this **Agni**, the digestive fire. When Agni is strong, food becomes energy, tissue, and vitality (Ojas). When it is weak, the same food becomes *Ama* — undigested residue that makes us tired, heavy, and prone to weight gain, bloating, and disease. Much of what we call "slow metabolism" is simply **weak Agni**, and it is remarkably fixable.

## Signs Your Agni Is Weak

- You gain weight easily, especially around the belly.
- You feel bloated and heavy after meals.
- You get sleepy after eating.
- You feel cold easily; hands and feet run cold.
- Poor appetite at meals, but cravings in between.
- A coated tongue, low energy, and sluggish bowels.
- Poor tolerance for "healthy" heavy foods — beans, dairy, rich foods.

If several of these ring true, the fix is not eating less — it is **digesting better**.

## The Fire-Kindling Routine

### On Waking

- **Warm water** — a glass on waking starts the fire.
- Add **lemon + a pinch of ginger** or a few **fresh mint leaves**.
- Do not drink iced water — cold quenches the fire.

### Before Meals

- A pinch of **ginger, cumin, and rock salt** taken with a little water 10 minutes before eating.
- Or chew a small piece of **fresh ginger** with a few drops of lemon.
- Sit down, breathe, and actually *begin* the meal with attention — the nervous system is part of digestion.

### During Meals

- **Warm, cooked food** — freshly cooked food carries the most prana (life energy); reheated and cold food digests poorly.
- Add **digestive spices**: cumin, coriander, fennel, black pepper, ginger, asafoetida (hing).
- Chew thoroughly — digestion begins in the mouth.
- Eat to **about 75% full** — the classical teaching is to leave a little room for the fire to work.

### After Meals

- A 5–10 minute **gentle walk** — the best digestive aid there is.
- A small piece of **jaggery with fennel seeds** or a pinch of **roasted cumin** in warm water.
- Avoid lying down immediately and avoid iced drinks.

## The Spice Shelf: Your Metabolism's Best Friends

- **Ginger** — the universal digestive stimulant; take as tea or fresh.
- **Black pepper (Maricha)** — kindles Agni and improves nutrient absorption.
- **Long pepper (Pippali)** — classically used to strengthen digestion; combines in *Trikatu*.
- **Cumin (Jeera)** — the most underrated metabolic aid; roast lightly and add to food or warm water.
- **Fennel (Saunf)** — digestion and after-meal freshness.
- **Turmeric (Haldi)** — supports the whole digestive system.
- **Asafoetida (Hing)** — reduces gas and bloating; a pinch in every cooking.

The classical formulation **Trikatu** (ginger + black pepper + long pepper) is the traditional metabolic trio — available as powder or tablets, used under guidance.

## The Two Metabolism Killers

### 1. Grazing and Snacking

Constant eating keeps the fire perpetually dampened. The body digests best with proper gaps between meals — **4–5 hours** — during which the previous meal is fully processed. This is not fasting; it is giving the fire time to complete its work.

### 2. Late Heavy Dinners

A heavy dinner at 10 PM is still being digested at 1 AM — when the body should be repairing, not processing. The classical rule: **make lunch the largest meal, dinner light and early** (by 7–8 PM).

## When Ama Is the Problem

If your fire is weak because of accumulated Ama — signs: coated tongue, heaviness, body ache, brain fog — then the first step is clearing, not just kindling:

- A **light-diet week** — moong dal soup and khichdi — to give digestion a rest.
- **Triphala** at night for gentle elimination.
- **Tongue scraping** each morning.
- Warm water throughout the day.

Then the fire can actually burn. Trying to nourish a system choked with Ama is like adding fuel to a clogged stove.

## The Bigger Picture

Agni is not only about food — it is the body's metabolic intelligence:

- **Sleep** — a tired body digests poorly. Sleep by 10:30 PM.
- **Stress** — the stressed nervous system diverts energy away from digestion. A daily breathing practice is a metabolism practice.
- **Movement** — daily activity literally warms the body and stokes the fire.
- **Sunlight** — morning sun is a metabolic anchor.

## How Long Does It Take?

Re-kindling Agni is a habit change, not an event. Within **1–2 weeks** of consistent warm-water, spice, meal-timing, and walking habits, most people notice less bloating and more energy after meals. Within **a month**, digestion feels visibly steadier, and weight management becomes noticeably easier — because the body is finally converting food into energy instead of storing it as Ama. That is the real definition of a working metabolism.
