Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) is quietly becoming one of the most common findings on routine health checkups. The liver — the body's chief metabolic engine — starts storing fat it cannot process. In Ayurveda, this maps closely to **Yakrit Medoroga**: fat tissue (*meda*) overwhelmed in the liver (*yakrit*), driven by weak Agni and accumulating Ama.

## Why Fat Collects in the Liver

The liver processes everything we eat, drink, and absorb. When it is overloaded by refined food, excess sweets, and sedentary habits, its digestive capacity is exceeded. The result is fat accumulation. Key drivers:

1. Refined carbohydrates and excess sugar — especially from soft drinks and sweets.
2. Deep-fried and "junk" food.
3. Alcohol — even moderate regular intake adds to the burden.
4. Being overweight, especially around the abdomen.
5. Insulin resistance and diabetes — the two conditions travel together.

## The Ayurvedic Approach to Fatty Liver

### The Three-Part Strategy

1. **Rekindle Agni** — the liver's metabolic fire is restored with warm, light, easy-to-digest food.
2. **Clear Ama** — gentle detoxification reduces the toxic load on the liver.
3. **Protect the tissue** — liver-supporting herbs and lifestyle that let the organ regenerate.

## Herbs Used in Classical Liver Care

- **Kalmegh (Andrographis paniculata)** — the bitter herb par excellence for liver support; used in classical *Bhunimbadi* formulations.
- **Bhringraj (Eclipta alba)** — traditionally used as a liver tonic and in fat-metabolism care.
- **Triphala** — gently supports digestion and elimination, reducing the load on the liver.
- **Kutki (Picrorhiza kurroa)** — a classical liver-protective herb; potent, so used in small doses under supervision.
- **Punarnava** — supports fluid balance and reduces congestion.
- **Amla** — antioxidant protection for liver cells.

**Note:** Many of these are strong herbs. They are best taken as classical formulations (e.g., *Arogyavardhini Vati*, *Phalatrikadi kwath*) under physician guidance rather than as random powders.

## The Diet That Reverses Fatty Liver

### Build the Plate

- **Half** — vegetables: leafy greens, bitter gourd, bottle gourd, broccoli.
- **Quarter** — whole grains: barley, millet, brown rice, oats.
- **Quarter** — protein: moong dal, tofu, legumes, small portions of lean protein.
- **Flavour** — turmeric, cumin, coriander, black pepper, ginger.

### Add Daily

- One teaspoon of **flaxseed** or soaked seeds.
- A glass of **warm water with lemon** in the morning.
- **Buttermilk** (chaas) with cumin and ginger after lunch.

### Remove

- Sugar, sweets, and soft drinks — completely.
- Refined flour and white rice in excess.
- Deep-fried food and excess oil.
- Alcohol — stop entirely during treatment.

## The Exercise Prescription

The liver responds to movement better than almost any organ:

- 45 minutes of brisk walking daily, or equivalent exercise.
- Strength training twice a week — muscle burns the sugar the liver cannot.
- Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutations) is an excellent starting point.

## Realistic Expectations

Fatty liver reversal takes **months, not weeks**. With disciplined diet and movement, meaningful improvement is often visible on ultrasound by 3–6 months, and many people fully resolve mild disease within 6–12 months. The earlier the stage, the better the outcome — which is why a routine liver panel and ultrasound are worth doing.

## When to Be Concerned

If your ALT/AST enzymes are significantly elevated, if you have diabetes, or if scans show scarring (*fibrosis*) rather than simple fat, you need a physician-led plan — including monitoring for progression. Ayurvedic care is a powerful ally here, but the liver's health must be tracked with proper investigations.
