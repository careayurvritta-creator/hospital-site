Prediabetes is the quiet warning stage when your fasting or post-meal sugar is higher than normal but not yet in the diabetic range. In classical Ayurveda, this stage corresponds to the early *Prameha* picture — Kapha accumulating, Agni weakening, and Ama beginning to clog the channels. The encouraging news: this is precisely the stage where lifestyle correction works best, and it is the stage Ayurveda is exceptionally good at addressing.

## What Is Happening in the Body

At this point, the body is still producing plenty of insulin, but the cells are beginning to resist its signal. Sugar lingers in the blood longer than it should. Ayurveda describes the same phenomenon differently: the digestive fire is too weak to properly process food, so the "half-digested" product circulates and eventually spills into the urine.

## The Five Early Warning Signs

1. Post-lunch drowsiness that forces you to rest.
2. Frequent urination, especially at night.
3. Unusual thirst and a dry mouth.
4. Recurrent skin infections, boils, or slow healing of small wounds.
5. A coated tongue and heaviness in the body, particularly after meals.

Not everyone will have all five — but even one or two deserve attention.

## Reversing Prediabetes: The Ayurvedic Plan

### Strengthen Agni first

- Begin the day with warm water — add a slice of fresh ginger.
- Make lunch the largest meal; keep dinner light and early.
- Chew thoroughly and eat in a settled environment.

### Clear Ama

- A short course of **Triphala** at bedtime supports digestion and regularity.
- Cooking with turmeric, cumin, coriander, and a little black pepper gently rekindles the digestive fire.
- One warm, lightly spiced *khichdi* day per week gives the gut a chance to reset.

### Move daily

A 40-minute daily walk after meals is one of the most effective — and cheapest — tools for reversing prediabetes. If you can, add a simple yoga sequence with Sun Salutations (Surya Namaskar) and breathing practice.

## Diet Corrections That Matter Most

### Add

- Fenugreek (methi) seeds — soaked overnight, taken on an empty stomach.
- Bitter vegetables — karela, bitter gourd, and leafy greens.
- Millets, barley, and whole moong dal in place of refined grains.
- Amla in any fresh form.

### Remove

- Sugar, sweets, and refined flour.
- Cold, sweetened drinks and ice cream.
- Day-time sleeping.
- Eating within three hours of bedtime.

## When Medication Is Needed

Not every case reverses with diet and lifestyle alone. If your sugar continues to rise despite disciplined habits, your physician may recommend medication — and that is the correct, protective step, not a failure. Ayurveda and modern medicine are partners here: one corrects the terrain, the other manages the numbers.

## The Bottom Line

Prediabetes is a gift — it gives you notice before the diagnosis. Use the window of opportunity deliberately. Measure your sugar, correct your digestion, move daily, and sleep well. With consistent effort, many people return to normal glucose tolerance and never cross the line into diabetes.
