When skin conditions persist for months or years, topical care alone rarely changes the pattern. This is where **Panchakarma**, the classical Ayurvedic system of purification and rejuvenation, becomes relevant. At Ayurvritta Ayurveda Hospital, skin-focused Panchakarma programs typically run for 7 to 21 days, depending on the depth of the condition and the strength of the patient.

## Why Skin Benefits From a Systemic Reset

In Ayurveda, chronic skin disorders belong to **Kushta**, and they are understood to involve aggravated doshas, weakened **Agni** (digestive fire), and accumulated **Ama** (metabolic toxins) lodged in the **Dhatus** (tissues), particularly the blood and plasma tissues. Creams and ointments work on the outer layer, but the supply line feeding the problem runs deeper. Panchakarma aims to empty that supply line, giving the body a chance to reset its own rhythm. It is not a weekend cleanse; it is a planned medical protocol with preparation, main procedures, and careful aftercare.

## The Three Stages of Care

Every Panchakarma program unfolds in three deliberate stages.

1. **Purvakarma:** preparation, which softens the body and mobilizes deep-seated doshas toward elimination.
2. **Pradhana karma:** the main purification procedures, selected according to the individual's dosha pattern and strength.
3. **Paschat karma:** aftercare, including a graduated return to normal food and long-term support.

Skipping preparation or rushing recovery undermines the whole effort, which is why the day count matters.

## What the 7 to 21 Day Framework Looks Like

The length of the program reflects how deep the imbalance runs.

- **Seven to ten days:** often suits milder or more recent conditions, and patients with lower strength, focusing on preparation, gentle purification, and external care.
- **Two weeks:** a common middle ground for established skin disorders such as psoriasis or chronic eczema, allowing full oleation, sweating therapy, and one main purification procedure.
- **Three weeks:** generally reserved for deep-rooted or widespread conditions, with extended preparation, comprehensive Shodhana (purification), and a longer recovery period.

During this time, therapies such as internal and external oleation, steam fomentation, medicated applications, and procedures like Vamana, Virechana, or Raktamokshana may be woven in, always chosen by the physician and never by default.

## What Daily Life Looks Like During Treatment

Patients are often surprised by how much of the protocol is quiet and simple.

- **Warm, light meals:** khichdi, soups, and gruels keep Agni steady while the body works.
- **Rest and routine:** early nights, gentle walks, and limited screen time support the process.
- **Oil and steam sessions:** daily therapies are soothing in themselves, and many patients sleep better during the program.
- **Skin support:** medicated oils and herbal pastes are applied to affected areas throughout the stay.
- **Close supervision:** the physician adjusts the plan day by day according to how the body responds.

## After the Protocol: Holding the Gains

The weeks after discharge matter as much as the treatment itself. Paschat karma typically includes a graded diet, herbs prescribed for the individual, and guidance on **Pathya-Apathya**, the foods and habits that favor or work against recovery. Many patients are also advised on **Ritu Charya** practices for seasonal stability and, where appropriate, **Rasayana** (rejuvenation) support to lengthen the time between flares. Relapses can still happen, but patients who follow aftercare generally experience longer calmer stretches.

## Who Should Use Care

Panchakarma is a medical procedure and is not right for everyone at every time. It is generally postponed during pregnancy, in young children, in the very elderly or frail, and during active fever, infection, or severe anemia. People with heart disease, uncontrolled diabetes, or serious chronic illness need thorough evaluation first. Never stop prescribed medication to undergo detox, and always disclose your full medical history. Choose a facility with trained physicians and proper clinical supervision rather than spa-style detox packages.
